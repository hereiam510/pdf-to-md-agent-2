## Selected F02-D03 failure-result consumption correction (2026-10-30)

### Outcome and selected method

Keep a malformed artifact local to its failed link/field so that independently established operation, version, request and attempt facts remain inspectable. The review reproduced `reference()` returning `status=unavailable, meta=null` and `_operation()` then raising AttributeError. The frozen-input consumer has the same source-supported pattern. This is a failure-result contract problem: the validator rejects the shape, but its caller consumes raw data as validated. It is not a new acceptance/recovery defect or evidence of real-task corruption.

Separate raw descriptor observations from validated facts in the diagnostic result contract. Use the existing small helper/projection structure; exact field/type names are implementation details. A failure result may retain raw values for explanation, but those values must not occupy a position that downstream code treats as validated structured metadata. Consumers must check the relevant status/fact availability before accessing structured fields or deriving ownership, labels or navigation. Do not merely replace null with `{}` and thereby manufacture a valid empty descriptor, drop the original value, or hide the actual failure reason.

Audit the bounded family of `reference()`/`artifact_facts()` consumers in diagnostics and the affected renderer: candidate/result links, frozen input, related/request links and any shared access to returned kind/meta/ownership facts. This is necessary to establish the same result boundary, not authority for a whole-repository validation redesign. Preserve the earliest applicable validation reason; a later ownership comparison must not overwrite a metadata-shape failure with an unsupported ownership claim. Additional independent failures may be shown separately when actually established.

On an unavailable candidate/result link, continue with independent inline operation/document/version evidence if its own checks succeed. ### Why the catalog is not already an export queue

[Store.put_tx](../../../src/pdfmd/storage.py) changes `data` on conflict but preserves `created`; there is no general record `updated` column. An exporter may use a complete on-demand projection, explicit state reconciliation/deduplication, or a separately justified change sequence. Neither a new durable spool nor schema changes are automatically necessary. ### Time and streaming semantics

Use wall timestamps for placement and monotonic measurements for duration within one invocation, recording the origin and whether a fact was observed, reconstructed or unknown. `attempt.started` precedes the receive clock's origin; adding a receive offset to it does not produce an exact wall timestamp. Parent/child spans overlap, so their durations are not additive task time. Receive elapsed includes client work and is not pure provider compute. Existing `ActiveTime` remains budget authority; a trace cannot repair unknown crash tails or rewrite recorded consumption.

In `Nexus.exchange`, `moment = elapsed()` is sampled for the yielded HTTPX body chunk **before** `record_chunk`, `decoder.feed` and `record_observation`. The same moment supplies that chunk's body/event/progress/terminal milestones even though classification and persistence happen later. The timestamp excludes its own chunk's persistence/decoding duration; earlier chunks' synchronous work and buffering can affect later observations. It is not a raw socket arrival or upstream first-token timestamp. Separate timers are needed to attribute persistence, decode and observation costs. Also distinguish transport bytes, effective generation progress and protocol terminal state; heartbeat traffic does not become model progress.

F03 timing cannot be a small change to `Dispatcher.settle` alone. `Documents.edit` commits its operation with the version/head and the dispatcher returns early; reconcile, skip, recovery and interrupted started operations also have distinct lifecycles. A logical operation duration, one fresh execution duration, receipt-reuse cost and an unknown interrupted tail are different fields/observations. Preparation timing should distinguish fresh assembly from frozen-request reuse without modifying the frozen input.

Prefer attempt-level counts and summaries over exported observations for every chunk. Existing durable raw chunks remain unchanged. Candidate additional measures include aggregate persistence/decode time and maximum observed inter-progress gap, with explicit definitions and measured overhead. Standard OTel batching exports finished spans; events attached to an unfinished long span do not by themselves guarantee live remote visibility. Keep local receive progress useful while offline.
