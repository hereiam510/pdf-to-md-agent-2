## Governing guarantees

Use [Q1](../../design/DOCUMENT_MODEL.md#q1-document-schema-and-commit), [edits and validation](../../design/DOCUMENT_MODEL.md#edits-and-validation), [commit/replay](../../design/DOCUMENT_MODEL.md#commit-and-replay) and [exact access](../../design/DOCUMENT_MODEL.md#exact-saved-document-and-evidence-access) as the governing rules. The diagnosis must preserve their distinctions:

- Source inventory, requested scope, output provenance and content claims are separate. A truthful complete physical-page map does not require transcribing excluded pages. Absence of a page update is not permission to classify that page outside scope; `unprocessed`, `outside_scope`, `omitted_non_content`, and printed-label `unknown`/`none` retain their meanings.
- Metadata updates merge with a named base before complete-result validation and the expected-head check. Existing prose, source characters, blocks, assets and unrelated metadata remain unchanged unless deliberately changed. Rejected candidates and immutable versions remain available; content reuse is not operation replay.
- Full/raw/field/range and native-pixel access, agent-selected methods and larger reconstruction remain available. Diagnose usability without imposing a compulsory script, preview, OCR stage, fixed field preset, hidden truncation or tool quota.
- A structurally valid `complete` claim, saved/reopened/exported consistency, source fidelity and natural agent completion require different evidence. Partial work remains usable and exportable.

The [document engineering guide](../../guides/DOCUMENT_ENGINE.md#implemented-editing-and-reuse-behavior) explains implemented mechanisms; its [page-map discussion](../../guides/DOCUMENT_ENGINE.md#6-page-maps-provenance-scope-and-claims) preserves rationale and historical alternatives.
