## Outcome and use


--- Original lines 6-14 ---

The agent completes conversion; the app provides reliable tools. The final product is an independent desktop app that starts its own agent, connects to configurable Chat Completions/Responses APIs and completes conversion without a Codex/ChatGPT interface. An external-chat/local-tool interface is also supported.

The user supplies a native, scanned or mixed PDF, range and desired outcome. Receive complete, readable, reusable Markdown with meaningful source images, working relative links and explicit limitations. Do not require routine page instructions, repair approvals, intermediate file transfers or manual output assembly.

Accept native/OCR text and image-only input without manual preprocessing in another app. The agent selects extraction, OCR, visual transcription, scripts and their combination, including OCR over corrupt or unhelpful text layers. It chooses useful scopes, regions, resolution, languages and reconstruction units. OCR is evidence, not authority over visible source content.

The product should improve user effort, reliable delivery, source handling, useful recovery or justified time/cost compared with ad hoc chat conversion. More tools, tests or documents alone do not establish this benefit.

## Content boundary

- Preserve substantive wording, names, values, units, punctuation, notation, meaningful emphasis, superscripts/subscripts and visible glyph distinctions. Do not silently correct source errors, paraphrase omissions, normalize Traditional/Simplified forms or invent uncertain text. Suggested corrections are separate.
- Preserve headings, paragraphs, lists, footnotes, substantive sidebars, reading order and within-/cross-page relationships. Reflow columns, wraps, spacing and fonts where meaning survives.
- Preserve table values, intentional blanks, headers, groups and meaningful merged-cell spans. No particular HTML/internal semantic schema is mandated; formatting limitations do not justify deleting relationships.
- Preserve complete meaningful figures/panels, captions, important searchable labels and relationships. Searchable transcription is separate from the faithful source graphic.
- Prefer omission of pure decoration, non-content colored furniture and redundant running headers/footers. Their omission alone must not trigger repair, model calls or delivery rejection. Unique headings, warnings, labels, footnotes and uncertain substantive marginal content are required or explicitly unresolved.
- Preserve source-supported visible distinctions. Pixels may not establish original Unicode encoding; use faithful transcription and uncertainty without guessing or demanding unavailable proof.

## Source images and cropping

Keep the supplied PDF unchanged. For complete extractable native images, preserve dimensions and original encoded data where available. Existing source JPEG is valid; do not add lossy recompression.

Cropping is allowed. Raster crops preserve retained source pixels without unintended resizing, color/depth reduction, painting, cleanup or AI redraw. Newly saved raster crops use lossless encoding. Separate thumbnails cannot replace delivered full-quality artwork.

Vector/composite figures and scanned-page regions may use faithful source renders/crops, identified as renders rather than byte-identical native files. Resolution must make required fine text/lines legible; PNG alone does not prove quality. Preserve source/page/region/render provenance. No arbitrary maximum pixel size, per-image approval or additional first-stage chart reconstruction requirement applies.

## Page markers

Markdown includes English HTML comments. PDF page numbers are one-based positions in the supplied file; printed labels come from visible page pixels, not bookmarks/viewer labels/assumed offsets. Preserve Roman and other printed forms.

```html
<!-- PDF page: 1 | printed page: none | note: cover spread -->

<!-- PDF pages: 5-6 | printed pages: unknown | section: preface -->

<!-- PDF page: 7 | printed page: iii -->
```

These are syntax examples. `none` means visibly absent; `unknown` means unconfirmed/unreadable. Notes are source-supported. Map every input page, including outside-scope and unprocessed pages. Grouped content uses accurate ranges/lists without implying unused intervening pages contributed.

Keep complete logical blocks intact; do not break sentences or tables to mark exact character-level boundaries. Comments are hidden in rendered reading and use the same correspondence as structured data.

## Saving and delivery

Support initial composition and local or larger reconstruction, including related pages, sections and tables. Preserve unrelated correct work, original sources and useful versions. Markdown, structured JSON, simple safe HTML preview and assets come from one saved version. Deliver a portable complete package with relative links.

The agent can inspect actual source/output images and saved reading. Task-workspace scripts support legitimate transformations ordinary operations cannot express, with documented execution/permissions. Isolate working data per document; export only intended content and referenced assets.

Useful partial work is an honest draft. Distinguish unprocessed scope, content uncertainty and technical failure. Known material errors or required unexamined scope prevent a complete-acceptance claim. Human review is optional, not a routine gate.

Record available elapsed time, usage and user/developer interventions. Unknowns are unknown. No telemetry framework or arbitrary cloud budget is a prerequisite; cancellation and declared operational limits are meaningful.
