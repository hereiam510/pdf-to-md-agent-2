`jre-full` is the 173-page supplied source; `jre` is a separate 14-page excerpt, each with its own manifest identity. The supplied folder/filenames say GRE, but the corrected Markdown's title says JRE; preserve the filenames in the manifest rather than silently renaming source facts. The provided original and `- Copy.pdf` are byte-identical; only one complete copy is included. The supplied filename includes `173无1-2`; 173 is the physical page count of this supplied PDF, not proof that every published page is present. Read visible printed labels rather than assuming an offset or filling absent pages.

The full JRE Markdown claims embedded OCR was used, but the supplied PDF has zero extractable characters on all 173 pages. Preserve that self-description as supplied data; do not inherit it as source provenance or app evidence. Its content, tiny graphics and page-marker completeness have not been independently recertified. `news-writing` has 290 pages with extractable characters; its Markdown's dual-OCR/page-verification claims are likewise unverified self-reports. Complete-source Markdown coverage and accuracy are not certified solely because it reaches late-book sections or has page comments.

Known limits:

- Traditional Chinese and English recognition were requested, but language-loading warnings mean successful loading of every requested language is not established. Producing OCR text does not settle that question.
- The referenced cover image is a rendered derivative saved as JPEG with added lossy encoding. It does not meet the current no-added-lossy requirement. Preserve it as reference data; obtain compliant artwork from the source PDF for a new conversion.
- The example does not establish complete recovery of tiny lettering inside miniature cover illustrations, nor independent verification of every transcribed character.

A focused check may use a short range from any source PDF here and generate new output through its actual tools. Comparing that output with an example can expose a question to inspect in the PDF; it cannot replace source checking or prove fidelity by matching the example. Use [ACCEPTANCE](ACCEPTANCE.md) for proportionate result checks. Consulted cases do not count as unseen documents for broader reliability claims.

File hashes, PDF page counts, link resolution and readable image files establish package integrity only. They do not certify all characters, table relationships, crops, printed-page mappings or graphics. There is no requirement to reproduce a case's exact method, add repeated review stages or exhaustively verify every example before useful delivery.
